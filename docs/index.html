04 / EVIDENCE CLASSIFICATION

What this page shows and what it does not establish.

NINE RECORDED CARDS / FOUR DOMAINS

Two Decision records, two HRTech records, two Health records, and three Legal cards are presented as public-safe projections from synthetic production-environment executions. The Legal representative card (G1) is one of five completed attempts in a separate fixed-payload batch; that batch's 5/5 completed, 0-authority-block result is recorded in full in the Legal projection, not just the one featured card.

SYNTHETIC PRODUCTION EVIDENCE

The source executions used synthetic inputs in the production environment. The public interface is a frozen replay.

PUBLIC-SAFE PROJECTION

Full execution identifiers and raw API responses are not published here. The page uses truncated public references.

NO UNIVERSAL BEHAVIOR CLAIM

These recorded cards do not establish identical behavior across every Portotify domain, intent, producer, or deployment.

NO INFERRED HRTECH, HEALTH, OR LEGAL VERDICT / CAPSULE

The observed HRTech, Health, and Legal responses did not expose explicit verdict or capsule metadata, so this page does not infer either for those records.

NO INDEPENDENT AUDIT OR SIGNATURE VERIFIER

The Decision projection records signature metadata, but this page provides neither independent signature verification nor an independent audit.

LEGAL BLOCKS ARE SEPARATE SYNTHETIC CONTRACTS

G2 and G3 each used their own synthetic contract text, distinct from the G1 batch payload and from each other. They are not paired A/B variants of a single request, and the rejected pre-block provider output was not preserved in the public-facing response for either case.

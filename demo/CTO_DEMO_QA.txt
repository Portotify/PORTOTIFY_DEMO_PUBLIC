Portotify: Demo Q&A (Technical Audience)

Q1. Does Portotify replace the decision-producing system?

No.

The producer may be an LLM, ML model, statistical model, algorithm, rule
engine, hybrid pipeline, or human-supported process.

Portotify governs the candidate decision rather than replacing its producer.


Q2. Is risk classification the governance decision?

No.

Risk classification is a deterministic governance signal.

A risk tier is not itself ALLOW, BLOCK, or REVIEW_REQUIRED.

Governance authority belongs to the component that forms the governance
verdict, not to a signal or score consumed by it.


Q3. What does ALLOW mean?

ALLOW means the candidate decision was admitted under the governance conditions
for that represented execution.

It does not mean permanent permission, universal authorization, regulatory
certification, or authority for another action.


Q4. What does BLOCK mean?

BLOCK means the candidate decision was rejected within the governed path
represented by that execution.

The producer cannot convert that BLOCK into ALLOW merely by asserting that it
has authority.

Portotify does not claim that a BLOCK controls unrelated external systems or
bypass paths outside the governed integration.


Q5. What is a Decision Capsule?

A Decision Capsule is a governance evidence artifact.

Public-safe capsule material can carry fields such as verdict, risk,
execution reference, policy or governance metadata, and lineage information.

The public repository does not expose every production field or every
production record.


Q6. Why isn't ordinary logging enough?

Logging and governance evidence solve different problems.

An ordinary application log may record requests, responses, errors, and
timestamps.

Governance evidence is intended to preserve the governance decision itself:
the verdict, its basis, relevant authority and policy context, and available
lineage.

A sufficiently designed logging system could carry some of this information.
Portotify does not claim that the word "log" makes such evidence impossible.


Q7. Is Portotify an AI-only governance system?

No.

The governance object is the consequential decision.

The producer may be generative AI, conventional ML, statistical, algorithmic,
rule-based, hybrid, or human-assisted.


Q8. Is regulatory mapping the same as compliance?

No.

Framework mapping is not certification, legal advice, or a conformity
assessment.

Classification and legal obligations depend on the actual system, use case,
role, and deployment context.


Q9. What problem is Portotify solving?

The authority gap.

Systems can produce plausible analysis, scores, recommendations, and decisions
without proving that those outputs have authority to become consequential.

Portotify creates a governance boundary between producing a result and allowing
that result to continue within a governed path.


One Sentence Summary

Portotify governs whether a candidate decision may become consequential within
a controlled path.

We govern the decision, not the decision-maker.
